Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects, how it is used, and how it is protected when you use the application (the "Service").

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide directly, such as your name and email address when you create an account, and the expense records you add — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to operate the Service: authenticating your account, storing your expenses, and generating the summaries and breakdowns you see in the app. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in the application's database and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly does not share your expense data with third parties. Where the Service relies on third-party infrastructure (such as hosting), those providers process data only as needed to run the Service and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided within the app.
